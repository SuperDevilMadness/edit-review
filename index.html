Review a video

Paste the link to the video. You get a page where you type notes on exact moments, mark stretches to cut, draw on the picture and, if you like, talk through it. Nothing is uploaded: your review is saved as a file you send back.

YouTube: public and unlisted videos work, private ones don't. Google Drive: you'll be asked to download the video first, because Drive doesn't let other pages play it.
