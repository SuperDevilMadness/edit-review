Edit Review and Edit review uploads · last updated 2026-11-20

The review page

The review page plays a video from a YouTube link or a file you pick. Notes you write, draw or record stay in your browser until you press Send or turn on shared notes. Voice notes are written out in your browser; the recording is not uploaded.

Shared notes and Send store your notes, the name you type and an anonymous sign-in id in Google Firebase, so the editor and others with the same video link can read them. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Edit review uploads (YouTube)

"Edit review uploads" is a private tool used only by the editor who runs this site. It asks for permission to upload videos to the editor's own YouTube channel, and uses it only to upload review renders as unlisted videos. It does not read, change or delete anything else on the channel, and it is not offered to other people.

The sign-in is kept on the editor's own computer and is never shared. You can remove the permission at any time in your Google account under Security → Third-party connections.
